# Clinical Labs Dashboard
Clinical Laboratory Dashboard
Your entire lab history, finally readable.
CLD turns years of scattered PDFs, portals, and paper results into one longitudinal record — every draw, every marker, tracked side by side so trends jump out instead of hiding across a dozen disconnected reports.
🤖
AI-assisted, not AI-replaced
Groq reads your reports and drafts a clinical narrative — priority flags, risk assessments, trend context — but every number stays traceable back to the original result.
📈
Real trends, not just snapshots
The 10-Year Health Arc and per-category tables plot every marker across every draw on file, with direction computed from your actual numbers.
🩺
Symptom Checker built in
A conversational assessment tool that saves every consultation, so a passing concern doesn't just disappear from memory.
🖨️
A report worth handing your doctor
Generate a clean, printable summary of exactly the sections you choose — no clutter, no app chrome, just the data.
🔒
Yours, and only yours
No server, no account, no sign-up. Everything lives in this browser; you decide when and where a backup goes.
Built for physician review, not self-diagnosis. Always bring questions and results to a licensed provider.
